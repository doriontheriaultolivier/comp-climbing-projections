# Boulder style-tag taxonomy

Current version: `2026-08-03.1`

Every optional tag is scored independently from 0 (absent) to 3 (dominant),
once for start→zone and once for zone→top.

## Active refinements

- Crimps/edges are split into 12–30 mm and under 12 mm.
- No-texture footholds are explicit.
- Generic toe-hook and heel-hook tags are replaced by far/close toe hooks and
  incut/smeary heel hooks.
- Generic compression is replaced by small/large sideways compression.
- Small/large sideways opposition is separate from compression.
- Pressing, undercling, reading/constraint and catch demands have explicit tags.

Older unsplit fields remain valid historical observations but must be labelled
`legacy_unsplit` in any future consensus build; they cannot be silently assigned
to one of the narrower categories.

## Candidates awaiting coach review

These are not active in the public form yet:

- pogo / foot-generated hand release;
- double-clutch or catch-and-release;
- 180°/360° rotation;
- cross-through / rose move;
- hand match or hand swap;
- foot swap;
- rock-over / high-step stand-up;
- bicycle;
- heel-toe cam;
- toe scum or calf scum;
- knee bar;
- stemming / opposing walls;
- campus / intentionally no feet;
- palm press / palm-down support;
- vertical compression;
- insecure slab step or slab pop;
- dual-texture handhold and dual-texture foothold.
