# Physical-support challenger contract V1

This contract freezes the first model comparison before human demand tags are
available. The baseline is chronological pre-event skill plus exact item
difficulty. Challenger one adds the two ordered Kilter-expression measures;
challenger two adds strongly shrunk, monotone physical-capacity-by-demand
support terms.

The phrase “physical ceiling” remains project shorthand only. The modeled
quantity is a probabilistic support surface for a tagged movement strategy, not
an absolute upper limit on the athlete. CES, soft-min, attempt hazards and any
competition-stress interpretation remain deferred.

Evaluation is chronological and competition-balanced. Zone and
Top-given-Zone log losses are co-primary; no literal observation-count gate is
used. Evidence strength is reported continuously through shrinkage/information
gain, event concentration, influence and tag disagreement. No training advice
or mental-state label is authorized by this contract.
