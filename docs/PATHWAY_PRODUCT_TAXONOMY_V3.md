# Pathway product taxonomy V3

The private Future Vision uses the final user-facing ladders:

- youth: **Y-NAT → Y-REG → YW-IFSC**;
- senior/open: **NAT → REG-IFSC → WC+**; and
- **OLY** as a conditional field-and-format scenario.

`REG-IFSC` replaces the ambiguous `REG` label. NACS, Pan-American, European,
Asian and other governed senior/open continental competitions share this broad
product head while retaining their event subtype. This does not imply that the
competitions have identical formats or difficulty.

The terminology separates demonstrated evidence from conditional readiness:
`WC+` means direct governed starts; `WC+ Ready` would mean performance
conditional on entry in a specified reference field. The latter remains
withheld until its pair, placement and uncertainty gates pass. The same rule
applies to `YW-IFSC Ready` and `REG-IFSC Ready`.

This release changes synthetic product vocabulary only. It does not alter or
promote any rating, probability, athlete estimate, interval or real-data model.
