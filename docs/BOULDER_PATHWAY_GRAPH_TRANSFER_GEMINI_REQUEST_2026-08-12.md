# Critical review request: worldwide pathway graph and transfer model

Review the attached revised pathway design as a skeptical senior Bayesian
ranking researcher and climbing high-performance director.

The owner proposes that every result influence every target rating through the
full athlete/competition graph, but with less effect when contexts share fewer
athletes, are farther in the pathway, or differ more in terrain difficulty. The
architecture now distinguishes federation, North-American inter-federation
(NACS), five recognised continental families, adult World, Youth World and an
Olympic scenario. Every family has a youth variant where it exists.

Questions that require a direct, critical answer:

1. Is shared time-varying skill plus partially pooled context response the best
   practical model under current data, or should we use another structure?
2. How should cross-context transfer depend on bridge athletes, uncertainty,
   pathway distance and future terrain distribution without double-counting or
   creating selection bias?
3. What hierarchy/covariance/kernel is identifiable with sparse Africa,
   Oceania, Pan America and NACS evidence?
4. Should NACS remain its own inter-federation context below Pan America?
5. How should youth/adult transfer be modeled without age leakage or treating
   Youth Worlds as adult World evidence?
6. Which ablations, chronological metrics and uncertainty diagnostics are the
   minimum credible test?
7. Critique the two display anchors (50% semifinal=2000, 50% final=3000) in this
   worldwide hierarchy.

Reject attractive but unidentifiable complexity. Distinguish what can be built
now from what should wait for Boulder Tags terrain evidence. Advice is advisory;
it cannot promote or deploy a model.
