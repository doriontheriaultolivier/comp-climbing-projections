# Physical support chronology alignment V1

This successor reconciles two compatible research lines that had evolved in
separate worktrees. The earlier physical-support chain already established the
governed data intake, identity bridge, competition timeline, exact-item bridge,
continuous estimability audit, uncertainty-preserving tag consensus and the
first preregistered model comparison. The later chronology review adds one
important boundary: a target competition's problem tags cannot be backdated
into a pre-event projection.

The earlier model ladder remains unchanged. The first comparison still asks
whether two dated Kilter-expression indicators improve chronological Zone and
Top-given-Zone forecasts beyond athlete history and item difficulty. A physical
capacity layer comes only afterward. A frontier or “ceiling” model remains
deferred. The monotone ordinal IRT suggested in the later review is a useful
auxiliary benchmark or parameterization, but it does not silently replace the
registered hurdle estimands.

There are now two explicit product clocks:

- A **pre-event projection** may use only athlete evidence and historical model
  information available before the target event. It cannot know the unseen
  target terrain.
- A **post-event coaching analysis** may use the observed target problems and
  their reviewed tags, with submission time and reviewer provenance. It may
  describe a conditional expression discrepancy, not diagnose pressure,
  motivation or a training prescription.

The next executable gate is therefore data work, not model fitting: materialize
the reviewed tag consensus, retain disagreement and timestamps, join only exact
item identities, and recompute continuous support by athlete, item, event and
reviewer. Until confirmed tag rows exist, the challenger stays preregistered and
inactive.
