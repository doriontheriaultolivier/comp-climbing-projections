# Physical coaching evidence unlocked by Boulder demand tags V1

The existing 535-item tag queue is now joined to the chronological physical and
Kilter-to-competition timeline. Every candidate item links to at least one
coaching observation; together they cover 20 athletes and 609 of 613 dated
observations. The queue contains no athlete identity or physical-test value.

The unlock rank answers one bounded question: which pending item tag connects
to the most distinct athletes and observations already available for later
support/transfer research? It does not estimate a style, a weakness, or a
training effect.

Twelve Boulder round contexts lack an item candidate. They are preserved in a
separate restricted gap table rather than receiving inferred tags. The largest
current gaps include several NACS/Senior National semifinal contexts and three
events from SAC-CAS/VL-GLOBAL.

All 535 rows remain `human_demand_tags_needed`; zero completed human demand tags
exist in the governed evidence chain. Shared-terrain evidence is competition
format metadata, not a substitute for a physical/style demand tag.

The next gate is independent human tagging, starting with high-unlock items,
followed by inter-rater agreement. No ceiling or transfer model should be fit
before that evidence exists.
