# Data and Analytics Access Terms

These terms supplement [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). They are a practical
contracting checklist, not a substitute for a signed agreement reviewed for
the relevant jurisdiction.

## Default position

Unless a signed agreement says otherwise, access is for private evaluation by
the specifically invited user only. It does not permit:

- redistribution of data, exports, screenshots, reports, or credentials;
- use in another product, team, federation, publication, or commercial
  service;
- automated extraction, bulk downloading, model training, or reconstruction
  of the underlying methodology;
- athlete selection or disciplinary decisions without qualified human review;
- publication of private testing, health, questionnaire, or video annotations.

## A permission agreement should define

1. the named organization and approved users;
2. the exact code, data, reports, or app features covered;
3. the permitted coaching, research, governance, or commercial purpose;
4. whether exports, publication, derivative analysis, and AI use are allowed;
5. data-security, privacy, safeguarding, retention, and deletion duties;
6. attribution, audit, incident-reporting, and termination rules;
7. fees, liability, governing law, and dispute handling.

## Public-source boundaries

Public competition facts remain public facts. This project adds value through
identity resolution, de-duplication, quality control, database design,
calibration, model implementation, visual design, and decision-oriented
interpretation. A contract can control access to those proprietary Materials
even where the underlying result was published elsewhere.

## Security

Do not place passwords, API keys, raw health data, private questionnaires, or
unreviewed personal annotations in the repository. Use deployment secrets and
named-user access. A demonstration password is not a substitute for identity-
based access.
